✦ AI pipeline

Our AI, out in the open

This isn't buried in a legal footnote. It's on the front page, in every store listing, and in every credits screen we ship.

⚡

Faster

Sprite sets that once meant a three-week freelance commission queue now iterate in hours. Concepts, recolors, and icon batches land the same day we ask for them.

💸

Cheaper

Manually hiring a full art team is far beyond an indie budget of our size — it would cost more than our entire production budget. Generative tools keep our games affordable and our studio independent.

🧘

Eases the workload

Repetitive production — recolor passes, icon variants, tiling textures — is exactly what models are good at. It eases the load so the team spends its energy on design, not drudgery.

🤖 Where AI helps
  • Sprite concepts & recolor variants
  • UI icon sets, frames & menu decoration
  • Tileable textures & background passes
  • Store-page mockups & marketing key-art drafts
🧠 Where humans lead
  • Game design, systems & balance
  • All gameplay code — written by people

  • Final art direction: every asset reviewed, edited & approved by hand
  • QA, accessibility & store publishing

Our commitments

  • Every AI-assisted asset passes a human review-and-edit pass before it ships.
  • AI use is disclosed on every store page and inside every game's credits screen.
  • We only use commercially licensed or self-hosted models, and we don't train on artists' work we don't have rights to.
  • Generated assets stay exclusive to Foxman games — we don't resell raw outputs.
  • Ask us anything: we'll tell you exactly which parts of a game were AI-assisted.
  • When revenue allows it, we intend to commission human artists for key art and cinematics.

Frequently asked

Do you use AI to write code or design levels?
No. Code, level design, and writing are human-made, full stop. Our AI use is limited to visual assets: sprites, UI, and textures — all human-reviewed before shipping.
Is this about replacing artists?
Honest answer: we're a three-person team, and a full-time art staff was never in the budget. Hiring manually for every sprite and UI set would cost more than our entire production budget. Generative tools are how a studio our size ships games that look finished. As revenue grows, we plan to bring human artists in for the work that matters most.
Which tools do you actually use?
Stable Diffusion (self-hosted, with our own fine-tunes) for sprite and UI concepting, Figma for UI layout, and Aseprite for hand-finishing. Full list in the credits section.
Can I use your game assets in my own project?
Only within the EULA: personal use, fan art, and content creation — yes. Extracting assets for other games, resale, or training AI models on them — no. Full details in the license section.
Where is this disclosed in-game?
In the credits screen of every release, plus a provenance note on each store page. If a build uses AI anywhere, the credits say exactly where.
