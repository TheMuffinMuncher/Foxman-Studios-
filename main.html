How generative AI fits into our games.

We believe players should be able to understand the tools used in the creative process. Here is our current production approach.

Public AI disclosure

Generative AI is used for selected production assets.

Foxman Studios uses generative AI to help create certain in-game elements, including sprites and user-interface (UI) assets. We use these tools because they can save development time and money, reduce repetitive work, and help avoid some production bottlenecks and headaches.

AI is a tool in the workflow — not the studio. The rest of our games, including their direction, design, programming, implementation, writing, and overall development, is made by real people. Where AI-assisted assets are used, they may be reviewed, edited, and integrated by the development team.

What AI may help with

Selected sprites, visual concepts, and UI elements used during game production.

What people do

Creative direction, game design, coding, level and gameplay implementation, writing, testing, and final decisions.

Why disclose it?

Transparency helps players make informed decisions and understand how a game was made.
